Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Print Vatika collects, uses, and protects information you provide when placing print orders.

Last updated: 21 August 2025

1. Who We Are

Print Vatika is a commercial printing press operating from F-298, Himmat Singh Marg, Near Saket Metro Station, Nai Basti, Lado Sarai, New Delhi – 110030 ("we", "us", "our"). We provide printing services including business cards, banners, t-shirts, flyers, and related products.

For privacy-related queries, contact us at 098114 27517 or via WhatsApp at the same number.

2. Information We Collect

When you place or enquire about an order, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery address: only if you select home delivery.
  • Design files: artwork, logos, or images you upload for printing.
  • Order details: product type, quantity, specifications, and pricing.
  • Payment reference: the UPI transaction reference number (UTR) you provide after paying. We do not collect or store card numbers, bank account details, or full UPI credentials.

We also collect non-personally identifiable information through standard browser storage (localStorage) on this website to maintain your cart and order history on your own device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Process and fulfil your print order.
  • Contact you via phone or WhatsApp regarding your order status, proofs, or delivery.
  • Resolve disputes or complaints relating to a specific order.
  • Maintain internal records required for GST compliance and business accounting.

We do not use your information for marketing emails, third-party advertising, or automated profiling of any kind.

4. Design Files and Artwork

Files you upload are used solely for producing your print job. We do not claim ownership of your artwork. Design files are retained for up to 30 days after order completion to handle reprint requests, after which they are deleted. We do not share your artwork with any third party.

5. Payments

Payments are made directly via UPI to our registered UPI handle. Print Vatika does not operate a payment gateway and does not collect or store any sensitive payment credentials. The UPI reference number (UTR) you provide is used only to verify receipt of payment.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Delivery partners: if you have selected Porter delivery, the delivery address and your first name may be shared with the courier for the purpose of delivery.
  • Legal obligation: if required by Indian law, court order, or a government authority.

7. Data Storage and Security

Order data entered on this website is stored in your own browser's localStorage — it does not leave your device unless you explicitly submit an order. Order records on our end are maintained in a secure internal system accessible only to Print Vatika staff.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information, but please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 098114 27517. We will respond within 15 business days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after any change constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
