Questions

Frequently asked

What file formats do you accept?
PNG, JPG, and PDF. For best results on large formats, send a high-res PDF or PNG at 150–300 DPI.
How does Porter delivery work?
Once your job is ready we'll notify you. You book a Porter pickup from our shop and schedule delivery to your location, or ask us to arrange it.
How fast is turnaround?
Business cards and small runs: same-day or next day. Large banners and bulk orders: 1–2 business days. Call us for urgent jobs.
Can I see a physical sample first?
Yes — come to our shop near Saket Metro. We'll show you paper stock, lamination, and banner material samples before you confirm.
Do you accept custom/unusual jobs?
Absolutely. Call or WhatsApp with your requirement and we'll tell you if we can do it and quote you quickly.
What payment methods are accepted?
UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash at the shop. Online orders are paid via UPI QR during checkout.
